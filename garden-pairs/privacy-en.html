Privacy Policy

Vườn Ghép Đôi / Garden Pairs · Dien Nguyen / CoderViet

2026-11-16 · Tiếng Việt

Overview

Garden Pairs (Vườn Ghép Đôi) is a tile-matching puzzle game developed by Dien Nguyen and published as CoderViet. This policy applies to the Android app, package com.diennk.gardenpairs, starting with version 0.2.3 (5) and later versions with the same privacy practices. All 13 levels in this version are free. No game account is required, and this Android version has no advertising or in-app purchases. This policy does not cover iOS test builds with different features.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your current board, level, score, remaining time, hints and shuffles, reward history needed to preserve game progress, and sound, music and vibration preferences in its private local storage. These records let you resume a game and remember your settings. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Data collection and sharing

This Android version does not transmit personal information or gameplay records to the developer or advertising providers. We do not use remote analytics or crash reporting, sell personal data, or track players across apps. Any optional local diagnostic events are kept in memory only and are not uploaded; diagnostics are disabled by default in the release build.

Advertising and other libraries

The Android app includes a shared Google Mobile Ads library, but advertising and consent requests are disabled in this version. The app does not initialize the ads service or request ads, its automatic ads initialization provider is removed, and advertising ID permissions are removed from the release manifest. Other bundled libraries provide local storage and playback of audio included with the app. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. If advertising or other data-handling features are enabled in a future version, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures before using them.

Retention, deletion and device transfers

Local game records remain on your device until you replace them through gameplay, clear app storage or uninstall the app. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Vườn Ghép Đôi → Storage → Clear storage/data. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove your saved game. There is no developer cloud save or game account to delete, and we cannot recover or remotely delete local saves. The app disables standard Android app backup. Device manufacturers or operating systems may separately offer device-to-device migration or restore services under their own settings and privacy practices; we do not receive or control those copies.

Children and other players

The game is intended for players of all ages. The privacy practices described here apply to every player, including children: the current Android app does not request an account or transmit personal information to us. If you believe a child or another person has sent personal information to us through a support message, contact us to request assistance or deletion.

Support correspondence

If you choose to contact khanhdien@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you send through our email provider. We use them only to respond and resolve the issue, not for advertising. Send only information needed for support and remove personal information from screenshots; never send passwords. We retain correspondence only as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable requirements.

Platform services and this website

Google Play, your operating system and email provider process information independently under their own privacy policies. This policy describes the game and our support practices, not those independent services. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses and request times when you visit, as described in its GitHub privacy statement. This site adds no analytics or advertising scripts, cookies or forms.

Security

The app relies on the operating system’s private app-storage protections for local game records. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider. No storage or communication method is completely secure; keep your device and accounts protected.

Changes

We will update this page and its revision date if these privacy practices change. Features with different data practices will be described in an updated policy and relevant store disclosures.
