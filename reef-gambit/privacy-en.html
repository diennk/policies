Privacy Policy

Reef Gambit · Dien Nguyen / CoderViet

2026-11-13 · Tiếng Việt

Overview

Reef Gambit is an offline naval puzzle game developed by Dien Nguyen and published as CoderViet. This policy applies to the mobile app, including Android package dev.reefgambit.reef_gambit. The game does not upload your gameplay or personal information to a developer-operated server and has no advertising. The first 10 stages are free; an optional one-time Full Game purchase unlocks stages 11–100 and practice mode. No separate game account is required; purchases and restoration use your platform store account.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your saved voyage, game progress, scores, gameplay statistics (such as moves and elapsed play time), language, audio and motion preferences in the app's private local storage. These records are used to resume your game, display your progress and remember your settings. They are not transmitted to a developer server.

Optional purchase and restoration

On Android, Google Play Billing processes the Full Game purchase and restoration. On iOS, Apple StoreKit performs these functions. The store processes your account and payment information under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your payment card details. The app receives product identifiers, purchase status and transaction/receipt information to verify ownership, deliver the unlock and acknowledge the transaction. Android purchase receipts (including the signed purchase data and purchase token) are stored locally in the app’s private storage so that purchased content can be played offline. Purchase and restoration require a store connection. We do not send these receipts to a developer-operated backend. You may restore ownership using the same store account. The platform may retain transaction records and make order information available to the developer through its store console; we use such information only for purchase support, refunds and transaction administration, not advertising. Refunds and payment issues are handled under the relevant store’s rules. Clearing app data does not cancel a completed store purchase.

Data collection, sharing and third-party code

We do not operate an app backend, collect identifiers or location, use advertising or analytics SDKs, sell personal data, or track you across apps. Bundled libraries provide local file storage, playback of audio included with the app, and store billing. Billing exchanges purchase-related information with the platform store for transaction processing; gameplay is not sent to us. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Reef Gambit → Storage → Clear storage/data to reset the app. Starting a new voyage replaces the current campaign but may retain your best score and preferences. Device or operating-system backup and restore features may retain or restore app data under your device/account settings. We do not receive or control those backups and cannot remotely access or delete your local saves. There is no app account to delete.

Children and other users

The app does not collect personal data from any player, including children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us, contact us so we can address the request.

Support messages

If you choose to email us, your email provider delivers the address and content you send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any records we are required to retain.

Platform services and this website

Google Play, your operating system, email providers and device backup services operate under their own privacy policies. Information processed independently by those services is not developer-operated app collection. This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard request information, including IP addresses, when you visit it, under GitHub's privacy statement. This site adds no analytics or advertising scripts.

Security

Game records use the operating system's app storage protections. We do not claim that local device storage or support email is immune from unauthorized access; keep your device and accounts secure.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if the app's privacy practices change. Future features that collect or share personal data will require an updated policy and disclosures before they are used.
