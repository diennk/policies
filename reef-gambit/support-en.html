Your voyage awaits

Reef Gambit is an offline, turn-based naval puzzle game. Move in eight directions, fire broadsides and outsmart enemy fleets. The first 10 campaign stages are free. A one-time Full Game purchase unlocks stages 11–100 and practice mode; no subscription. Gameplay is offline after unlocking, while buying or restoring requires a connection to the store.

Get help

Email khanhdien@gmail.com with the app version, Android/iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending. Never send passwords.

Save data

Game progress is saved locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove your saves. Practice mode preserves your campaign. We cannot retrieve a save from a developer server.

Restore Full Game

Use the same Google Play or Apple account used for the purchase. Open Unlock full game and choose Restore purchases. We cannot transfer ownership between Google Play and Apple accounts. For billing or refund questions, contact the platform store or email us with the order reference; never send payment card details.
